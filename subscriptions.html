Subscriptions and Refunds Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

This policy applies to ViralTap Pro, offered by MEDIAPRONET SRL, Republic of Moldova. It forms part of our Terms of Use. Pro is not on sale yet, and you cannot buy it in the current version. This policy will apply once it goes on sale.

What Pro includes

  • Unlimited videos
  • No watermark or end card
  • All styles, voices and backgrounds
  • Full HD 1080p, on supported phones
  • Batch video making
  • Custom colors, fonts and caption looks

Free features remain available without paying, within the daily limit shown in the app.

Plans and prices

Plans may include weekly, yearly and lifetime options. The price, currency and billing period appear in the app and on the Google Play payment screen before you confirm. The Google Play screen is the final price, including any tax it shows.

Payment

All purchases are made through Google Play, using the payment method on your Google account. We do not see or store your card details.

Automatic renewal

Weekly and yearly plans renew automatically at the end of each period, and Google charges your payment method, unless you cancel before the renewal date.

A lifetime plan is a single payment and does not renew. It stays with your Google account for as long as we offer the app.

How to cancel

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile icon, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose ViralTap and tap Cancel.

Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. After you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Price changes

If a subscription price changes, Google Play will notify you as its rules require, and you can cancel before the new price applies.

Refunds

Google Play handles payments, so refund requests usually go through Google. You can ask within Google's refund window through your Google Play order history. After that window, you can contact us and we will look at your request fairly.

We do not normally refund part of a subscription period, unless the law requires it.

If you live in the European Union, the United Kingdom or another place with a withdrawal right, you may have a right to withdraw from a purchase of digital content within 14 days. You may lose this right once you agree to immediate access and the service starts. Your mandatory consumer rights are not affected by this policy.

Restoring a purchase

Purchases are linked to your Google account. On a new phone, sign in with the same Google account and tap Restore purchase on the Pro screen.

If something goes wrong

Please email us if Pro does not unlock or a purchase looks wrong, and include your Google Play order number if you can.
