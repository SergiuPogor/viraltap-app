Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how the ViralTap Android app handles information. ViralTap is provided by MEDIAPRONET SRL, a company registered in the Republic of Moldova with IDNO 1012600005500 ("we", "us"), represented by its CEO, Serghei Pogor. Contact: mediapronetsrl@gmail.com.

The short version

ViralTap makes videos on your device. We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The app does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send your videos, text, photos or settings anywhere.

Information that stays on your device

The following stays in the app's private storage on your phone, and we cannot access it:

  • Videos you make
  • Text you type into the app
  • Your handle, if you add one
  • A list of content already used, so that videos do not repeat
  • Your settings, such as voice and speed, a count of how many times you have shared, and your daily streak

You control this information. Deleting a video in the app removes it, and uninstalling the app removes everything above. The app does not include this information in Android backups. Videos you save to your gallery stay there until you delete them.

Photos and videos you choose

If you add your own background, Android's photo picker gives the app access only to the item you choose. The app makes a temporary copy in its private storage to build your video. It deletes the copy once the video is made, or within a day if the video is not finished, so that you can try again. The app cannot browse your gallery.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Android sends your video to the app you pick. When you post content to another service, that service's own terms and privacy policy apply. Suggested captions are copied to your device clipboard so that you can paste them.

Text-to-speech

Narration uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone, which comes from your device maker or Google. The app offers only voices that work offline, and it sends your text to that engine on your device.

Google Play

We distribute ViralTap through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update apps, as described in its own privacy policy.

  • Reviews: if the app asks for a rating, Google Play shows that dialog and handles your response.
  • Crash and performance statistics: if you have allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics, Google may show us these as totals. We cannot use them to identify you.
  • Purchases: Pro is not on sale yet. If it goes on sale, Google Play will process the payment and we will never see your card details. Google will confirm to the app that a purchase is valid, and we may receive order reports from Google containing order details needed for accounting, tax and customer support.

Content updates

All templates and content are included in the app. New content arrives only through app updates from Google Play.

Permissions

The app does not ask for your camera, microphone, contacts, location or internet access. It uses these permissions:

  • Foreground service: keeps your video rendering if you switch apps. It runs only after you tap Generate.
  • Network state, wake lock and run at startup: added by the Android library that runs background work, so that a task can finish reliably. Network state only lets that library check whether a connection exists. It does not give the app internet access.

This website

This website sets no cookies and has no analytics. Our hosting provider may briefly process your IP address in server logs to deliver the pages and keep them secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority. As we hold no personal data from the app, most requests can be met by deleting videos in the app or uninstalling it. If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you, or taking steps you ask for. We keep the message only as long as needed for that purpose or as the law requires.

You can contact us about any privacy question at mediapronetsrl@gmail.com. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live or work. In the Republic of Moldova this is the National Center for Personal Data Protection.

Children

ViralTap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Security

Your content stays in the app's private storage, protected by Android. Please keep your device secure. Content you share or save outside the app is outside our control.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
