From idea to posted in three taps

No camera, no editing timeline and no blank page. Pick what kind of video you want and the app does the rest.

  1. Pick a format

    Choose a quiz, story, poll, Top 5 or Fact or fake, then a topic. Or tap Surprise me.

  2. Tap Generate

    ViralTap writes the scenes, reads them aloud, times the captions to each word and adds music.

  3. Share it

    Send it to the app you post on or save it to your gallery. A caption and hashtags are ready to paste.

Questions

Is ViralTap free?

Yes. You can make up to 10 videos a day for free. Free videos carry a small ViralTap badge and end card. A Pro plan is planned but is not on sale yet.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. Videos are made entirely on your phone, so you can create offline as long as your phone has an offline voice installed. You only need a connection to post the video in another app.

Can I post the videos and earn from them?

Yes. You may use videos you make for personal and commercial purposes, including monetised posts. You are responsible for any text or media you add yourself. See the Terms of Use.

There is no voice, or it sounds wrong

Voices come from your phone. In the app, open Settings and tap Install or manage voices to add an offline voice.

Where are my videos saved?

In the app's library. Videos you save to your phone appear in your gallery in the Movies/ViralTap folder.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is on your phone and there is no account to close. Delete videos in the app, or uninstall it to remove everything.
