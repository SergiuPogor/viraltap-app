Support

Need help? Email mediapronetsrl@gmail.com. It helps to include your phone model, your Android version and the app version, which is shown in Settings.

There is no voice, or it sounds wrong

Voices come from your phone. Open Settings in the app and tap Install or manage voices to add an offline voice.

Where are my videos?

Videos you make are in the app's library. Videos you save to your phone are in your gallery, in the Movies/ViralTap folder.

I reinstalled the app or changed phones and my videos are gone

Videos in the library are stored only on your phone and are not backed up. Save or share any video you want to keep.

Why can I not make more videos today?

The free version has a daily limit, which resets the next day.

How do I delete my data?

All of your data is on your phone, and there is no account to close. Uninstall the app to remove it, and delete any saved copies from your gallery. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report a problem with content?

Email us with the format and the text you saw, and we will review it. To report a copyright or other legal concern, email us with the details.
